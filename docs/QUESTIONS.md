# 10 questions for the research agent

Ten project-specific user questions are listed below. Questions 1, 3, 5, and 8 require comparing information across documents or pages. Question 10 is deliberately outside the corpus. Full report PDFs are not yet collected, so page-level evidence for questions 1–9 remains pending.

| # | Question | Where the answer is (document, page) | Needs more than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How did the number and value of bank-reported frauds change from the previous year? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | Yes |
| 2 | What was the total value of frauds reported by banks in the latest annual report? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | No |
| 3 | How has the share of card and internet frauds in total reported fraud cases changed over the last five years? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | Yes |
| 4 | Which bank groups reported the largest number of fraud cases in the latest reporting year? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | No |
| 5 | How have digital-payment fraud cases and amounts changed across the last five annual reports? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | Yes |
| 6 | What cybersecurity risks or vulnerabilities does the latest Financial Stability Report highlight for the financial sector? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | No |
| 7 | What does the latest Financial Stability Report say about the resilience of banks to cyber incidents? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | No |
| 8 | Which RBI measures related to payment security were introduced or updated, and what changes do the reports describe afterward? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | Yes |
| 9 | What fraud-prevention or cyber-risk steps should an urban co-operative bank consider based on the RBI reports? | Pending corpus collection and page mapping | Yes |
| 10 | Was a FraudPulse user's individual bank account involved in a fraud reported yesterday? | Cannot be answered from RBI publications; requires private bank/customer records | No |
